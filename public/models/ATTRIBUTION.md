# Human scan model

`head.glb` is the unmodified **Infinite, 3D Head Scan** by **Lee Perry-Smith / Infinite Realities**, sourced from the Three.js example assets.

- Source: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/models/gltf/LeePerrySmith
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
- Original work: https://www.triplegangers.com/

DepShield renders the mesh as sampled particles and a wireframe; no photograph or screenshot is used in the hero. The model represents a decorative scan visualization, not actual security telemetry.
